## How do I transfer tickets to someone else

Open your order in the app or on the website, choose Transfer, pick the
tickets you want to send, and enter the recipient's email address. They get an
email with a link to accept. Transfers are free, and you can transfer any
number of tickets from an order. The recipient does not need an existing
account; accepting the transfer creates one. A support agent can also start a
transfer for you over the phone after reading the details back for your
confirmation.

## Is there a deadline for transferring tickets

Transfers must be accepted before the event start time. You can send a transfer
right up until doors open, but if the recipient has not accepted by the event
start time the transfer expires and the tickets return to you. For events with
timed entry, the deadline is the start of your entry window rather than the
event start. We recommend sending transfers at least 2 hours ahead so the
recipient has time to accept and download them.

## Can I cancel a transfer I already sent

Yes, as long as the recipient has not accepted it yet. Open the order, find the
pending transfer, and choose Cancel transfer. The tickets return to your
account immediately and the recipient's link stops working. Once a transfer has
been accepted it cannot be reversed by us, because the tickets now belong to
the recipient. At that point the only way to get them back is for the recipient
to transfer them to you.

## Can I transfer will-call tickets

Will-call orders cannot be transferred, because the tickets are released at the
box office against the buyer's photo ID. If you need someone else to collect a
will-call order, the original buyer must contact support before the event to
change the name on the order. Name changes are free, take effect
immediately, and can be made up to 2 hours before doors open. Mobile and
print-at-home orders transfer normally, so if you expect to need to hand
tickets on, choosing mobile delivery at checkout is the simpler option.

## What happens to my tickets after I transfer them

Once the recipient accepts, the tickets disappear from your account and appear
in theirs. Your original order record stays visible in your history, marked as
transferred, so you keep the receipt. If the event is later cancelled, the
refund goes to you as the original purchaser, not to the recipient, because the
payment came from your card. The same applies to a postponement refund, and it
is worth telling the recipient this so they are not waiting for money that is
coming to you instead.
