## Is there a minimum age for events

Age limits are set by the organizer and vary by event. Most shows are all ages,
but club shows at The Foundry are 18 and over, and some late events at Lumen
Hall are 21 and over. The limit for your event is shown on the event page under
Event information, and it is also printed on your ticket. Age limits are
enforced at the gate, and there is no refund for being turned away on age.

## What ID do I need to bring

For age-restricted events bring a government-issued photo ID showing your date
of birth: a passport, a driving licence, or a national identity card. Photos
of an ID, expired documents, and student cards are not accepted. For will-call
collection you need photo ID matching the name on the order, but the date of
birth does not matter unless the event is age-restricted as well.

## Can under-18s attend with an adult

At all-ages events children of any age need their own ticket, except that
infants under 2 may sit on a lap without a ticket at Harbour Pavilion and
Lumen Hall. At 18-plus events no one under 18 is admitted regardless of who
accompanies them. A small number of events are 16-plus with an accompanying
adult over 21, and the event page says so explicitly where that applies.
