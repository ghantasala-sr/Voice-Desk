## Where are my tickets

Mobile tickets appear in the app under Upcoming events. For most events they
are released 48 hours before doors open, so if your event is further away than
that the order will show as confirmed but the tickets will not be downloadable
yet. Open the app and pull down to refresh if you expect them and do not see
them. Will-call orders never appear in the app, because they are collected at
the venue box office instead.

## My tickets are not showing in the app

First check that you are signed in with the same email address that is on the
order, since tickets follow the account that bought them. Then confirm the
event is within 48 hours, because tickets are not released before then. If both
are true, sign out and back in to force a refresh. If they still do not appear,
a support agent can resend them to the email on the order, which gives you a
backup link that works without the app.

## How does will-call pickup work

Bring photo ID matching the name on the order to the venue box office. Will-call
windows open 90 minutes before doors at most venues. The card used to buy the
tickets is not required. Only the named purchaser can collect, so if someone
else needs to pick them up the purchaser must contact support in advance to
change the name on the order. Name changes are free and take effect
immediately.

## Can I print my tickets at home

Print-at-home is available only where the organizer has enabled it, and the
option appears on the order page if it is. Printed tickets are scanned from
paper at the gate and each barcode admits one person once. Do not share a photo
or screenshot of a printed ticket, because whoever scans that barcode first
gets in and the rest are turned away. Mobile tickets are more secure and are
the default for most events.

## When are tickets delivered before the event

Mobile tickets are released 48 hours before doors open. Will-call is available
from 90 minutes before doors. Print-at-home links are sent at the same 48-hour
mark as mobile. For events with timed entry, the 48-hour release is measured
from the start of the event, not from your individual entry window. Buying
inside the 48-hour window delivers tickets within about 15 minutes of purchase.
