## How will I know if my event is cancelled

We email every ticket holder at the address on the order as soon as the
organizer confirms a cancellation, and the order page updates at the same time.
We also send a push notification if you have the app installed. Announcements
can come at short notice, sometimes on the day of the event, so it is worth
checking the order page before you travel. If you are unsure whether an email
is genuine, open the app rather than following the link.

## What are my options if an event is postponed

Postponed events keep your original tickets valid for the new date, and you do
not need to do anything. If you cannot make the new date, you can request a
refund within 30 days of the new date being announced. After that 30-day window
your tickets stay valid for the rescheduled event but are no longer refundable.
If a new date has not been announced yet, the refund window does not start, and
you can wait to decide.

## What if the event ends early or is cut short

Events that start and are then stopped are handled case by case by the
organizer, and the outcome depends on how much of the event took place. There
is no automatic refund in this situation. A support agent will file a request
on your behalf and the organizer decides. Please allow up to 14 days for a
decision after a shortened event, since the organizer usually reviews all
affected orders together.

## Is a venue change treated like a cancellation

A change of venue within the same city keeps your tickets valid and is not
treated as a cancellation, although your seat numbers may change. If the event
moves to a different city, you can request a refund within 30 days of the
announcement, the same as a postponement. Seat changes caused by a venue move
are allocated by the organizer, and we cannot choose specific replacement seats
for you.
