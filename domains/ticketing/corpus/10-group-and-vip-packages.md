## Do you offer group rates

Groups of 10 or more qualify for group pricing on most events, which is
typically 10 to 15 percent below the published face value and waives the
per-ticket service fee. Group orders are placed by our group desk rather than
on the website, and a deposit of 25 percent holds the seats for 14 days. Group
rates are not available on resale tickets, VIP packages, or events the
organizer has excluded, and the event page notes any exclusion.

## What is included in a VIP package

VIP contents are set by the organizer and differ by event, but a standard VIP
package includes a premium seat, early entry 60 minutes before general doors,
a dedicated entrance, a merchandise item, and access to the VIP lounge with a
complimentary drink. Meet-and-greet access is a separate higher tier and is not
part of standard VIP. The exact contents for your event are listed on the event
page under Package details.

## How do I book for a group

Email the group desk or ask a support agent to raise a group enquiry with the
number of tickets, the event, and your preferred price band. The group desk
replies within 2 business days with a quote and a hold. Holds last 14 days from
the quote, and the 25 percent deposit is due within that window. Final payment
is due 30 days before the event, or immediately if the event is inside 30 days.

## Can I book a suite or hospitality box

Suites are available at Harbour Pavilion, Northgate Arena, and Lumen Hall, and
seat between 8 and 20 people depending on the venue. Pricing is per suite per
event rather than per ticket, and includes catering at Northgate Arena only.
Suites are booked through the group desk with the same 14-day hold and
25 percent deposit. Kestrel Amphitheatre and The Foundry have no suites.
