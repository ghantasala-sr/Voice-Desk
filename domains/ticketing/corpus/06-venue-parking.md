## Is there parking at Harbour Pavilion

Harbour Pavilion has a 600-space garage attached to the venue, at 25 dollars
for event parking, payable by card only. The garage opens 2 hours before doors
and is usually full within an hour of opening on sold-out nights. There is no
pre-booking. Street parking around the harbour is metered until 10 pm and is
strictly enforced. The garage exit is single-lane, so expect 30 to 40 minutes
to clear it after a full house.

## Is there parking at Kestrel Amphitheatre

Kestrel Amphitheatre has free general parking in three lots holding about 2,000
cars between them, and the lots open 3 hours before doors. Preferred parking
closest to the gate is 40 dollars and can be bought in advance on the event
page. The lots are unpaved and can be muddy after rain. Lot exits merge onto a
single access road, so leaving after a sold-out show commonly takes 45 minutes
or more.

## How does accessible parking work

Every venue reserves accessible spaces in its closest lot or garage level, and
these cannot be reserved in advance. Display a valid accessibility placard or
plate, and arrive early, since the spaces are first come first served. At
Harbour Pavilion the accessible spaces are on garage level 1 and cost the same
25 dollars as general parking. At Kestrel Amphitheatre accessible parking is
free, in the same way as general parking.

## Where do rideshare cars pick up

Harbour Pavilion has a dedicated rideshare zone on Ferry Lane, a 3-minute walk
from the north exit. Kestrel Amphitheatre uses the Lot C entrance. Northgate
Arena uses the corner of 9th and Bell. The Foundry and Lumen Hall have no
dedicated zone, so drivers collect from the nearest side street. Surge pricing
after a sold-out event is common, and waiting 20 minutes usually costs less
than leaving immediately.
