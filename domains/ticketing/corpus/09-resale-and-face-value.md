## How do I resell tickets I cannot use

Open the order, choose Sell, and set your asking price. Listings go live
immediately and stay up until the event starts or you remove them. You keep
your tickets until they sell, so you can withdraw a listing at any time. When
a listing sells, the money reaches your bank account within 5 to 7 business
days after the event, not immediately after the sale, because the funds are
held until the event takes place.

## Is there a limit on resale prices

Resale prices on our marketplace are capped at face value plus 10 percent for
events where the organizer has enabled a price cap, which covers most but not
all events. Where no cap applies you can set any price. The cap is based on the
original face value, not on what you paid including fees, so a capped listing
may be lower than your total order cost. The event page states whether a cap
applies before you list.

## Are resale tickets guaranteed

Tickets bought through our marketplace are the same barcodes as the original
order, reissued to you, so they scan at the gate exactly like any other ticket.
If a resale ticket fails to scan we refund it in full. Tickets bought on
third-party resale sites are outside our system, we cannot verify them, and we
cannot help if they do not work. Resale tickets cannot be exchanged, but they
can be transferred and resold again.

## What fees apply when I sell

The seller pays a 10 percent fee on the sale price, deducted before the payout
reaches your bank. The buyer pays the standard per-ticket service fee on top of
the listing price. There is no fee to create a listing or to remove one that
has not sold. If the event is cancelled after a resale, the buyer is refunded
and the seller's payout is reversed.
