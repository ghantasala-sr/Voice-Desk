## What payment methods do you accept

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do
not accept cash, cheques, prepaid gift cards, or bank transfer. One order can
use only one payment method, so an order cannot be split across two cards. Gift
cards issued by Tide and Tempo Tickets can be used for the ticket price and the
service fee, but not for will-call delivery fees.

## What are the service fees on my order

Every order carries a per-ticket service fee and a single per-order processing
fee. The per-ticket fee is set by the organizer and varies by event, typically
between 8 and 18 percent of the face value. The per-order processing fee is
3 dollars and 50 cents regardless of order size. Both are shown separately
before you confirm payment and on your receipt. Will-call delivery adds a
further 5 dollars per order.

## I think I have been charged twice

A second charge of the same amount is usually an authorization hold rather than
a real charge, and holds normally drop off within 3 to 5 business days without
any action. If you have two separate order confirmation emails with different
order numbers, that is a genuine duplicate order and a support agent can file
it for review. If you believe a charge is fraudulent or you have already
disputed it with your bank, the call goes to a human specialist.

## Why was my card declined

The most common reason is a mismatch between the billing postcode on the order
and the one your bank has on file. Other common causes are a daily spending
limit, a card not enabled for online use, and a fraud block triggered by a
high-value ticket purchase. We never see the reason your bank gave. Your bank
can usually clear a block in a few minutes by phone, after which the order can
be placed again.

## How do I get a receipt

A receipt is emailed when the order completes, and every order page has a
Download receipt button that produces the same document as a PDF. Receipts show
the face value, the per-ticket service fee, the per-order processing fee, and
any delivery fee as separate lines. We cannot change the name or address on a
receipt after purchase, because it reflects the billing details the payment was
authorized against.
