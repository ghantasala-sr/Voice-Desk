## How do I buy accessible seating

Accessible and wheelchair-space seats are sold on the event page in the same
way as general seats, and you do not need to call ahead or provide
documentation. Filter the seat map by Accessible to see what is available. Each
wheelchair space is sold with one companion seat beside it. If the filter shows
nothing available, the accessible inventory for that event is sold out, and a
support agent can check whether the organizer is holding anything back.

## Can I bring a companion or carer

Every wheelchair space includes one companion seat at the same price, sold
together automatically. If you need more than one companion seated with you, or
you need a carer ticket at no charge, that is decided by the organizer rather
than by us, and a support agent will pass the request to them. Companion seats
cannot be bought separately from the wheelchair space they belong to.

## Are service animals allowed

Trained service animals are welcome at all our venues and do not need to be
registered in advance. Emotional support animals, therapy animals, and pets are
not permitted. Your service animal must stay under your control and in your
own seating space rather than in the aisle. Relief areas are available outside
every venue, and gate staff can direct you to the nearest one.

## Are assistive listening devices available

Harbour Pavilion, Northgate Arena, and Lumen Hall provide assistive listening
receivers free of charge from guest services, with a photo ID held as a
deposit. Kestrel Amphitheatre and The Foundry do not. Captioning and sign
language interpretation are arranged by the organizer, not by us, and requests
for them need to reach the organizer at least 14 days before the event.
