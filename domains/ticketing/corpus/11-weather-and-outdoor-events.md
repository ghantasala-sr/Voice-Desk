## Does rain cancel an outdoor event

Outdoor events go ahead in rain. Kestrel Amphitheatre and all our festival
sites operate rain or shine, and there is no refund for weather that does not
stop the event. If the organizer cancels for weather before the event starts,
the full cancellation policy applies and every ticket is refunded
automatically. If the event starts and is then stopped for weather, the
organizer decides case by case, usually within 14 days.

## What happens in severe weather

If lightning is detected within 8 miles of an outdoor venue, the site is
evacuated to the parking lots and the event is paused for at least 30 minutes
from the last strike. Follow staff instructions and return to your car rather
than sheltering under trees. Events usually resume if the delay is under 90
minutes. Updates are sent by push notification and posted at the gates, and the
decision rests with the organizer and venue safety team.

## What can I bring if rain is forecast

Umbrellas are refused at every venue in all weather, because they block the
view of people behind you. Ponchos and rain jackets are allowed and are usually
on sale at the merchandise stands. Small blankets are allowed at Kestrel
Amphitheatre but not at indoor venues. Lawn seating at Kestrel is grass, which
gets muddy quickly, so sealed footwear is a better choice than open sandals.
