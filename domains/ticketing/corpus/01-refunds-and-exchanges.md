## Are tickets refundable if I change my mind

Tickets are non-refundable once the order is complete. This applies whether
you can no longer attend, bought the wrong date, or changed your mind about
the event. The only exceptions are an event cancelled by the organizer and an
event postponed to a date you cannot make. If you cannot attend, you can
transfer your tickets to someone else at no charge, or list them on our resale
marketplace. A support agent can file a refund request for review, but filing
a request is not an approval and no refund is guaranteed.

## What happens if the organizer cancels the event

If an event is cancelled by the organizer, every ticket is refunded
automatically to the original payment method. The refund includes the ticket
price and all service fees. You do not need to contact us or return anything.
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days of the cancellation
announcement, and your bank may take a further 3 business days to post it.
If the original card is closed or expired, the refund still goes to that card
and your bank will route it to your current account.

## Can I exchange my tickets for a different date

Exchanges are available only for events that run on multiple dates, and only
where the organizer has enabled exchanges. When available, you can exchange up
to 7 days before your original event date, and you pay any difference in price.
There is no exchange fee. Exchanges are not available for single-date events,
sold-out dates, or resale tickets. If an exchange is not available for your
event, transferring your tickets to someone else is usually the fastest option.

## How long does a refund take

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days. The money returns
to the original payment method, and your bank typically posts it within a
further 3 business days. You will get an email when the refund is sent. If more
than 10 business days have passed since that email and you still do not see the
money, contact your bank first with the refund confirmation number from the
email, since at that point the delay is on the banking side rather than ours.

## Can I get a refund on service fees

Service fees are refunded in full whenever the ticket price is refunded. That
covers cancelled events, postponed events where you choose a refund, and any
refund request a support agent approves. Service fees are not refunded on a
voluntary transfer or a resale, because the original order stays valid and the
tickets simply move to someone else. Delivery fees for will-call orders are
also refunded with the ticket price.
