## What size bag can I bring in

Most of our venues allow one bag per person up to 12 inches by 12 inches by
6 inches. Clear bags are not required. Backpacks of any size are refused at
Harbour Pavilion and Northgate Arena, but are allowed at Kestrel Amphitheatre
if they fit the size limit. Oversized bags cannot be left at the gate, so if
yours is too large you will have to return it to your car or to off-site
storage before you can enter.

## What items are not allowed inside

Outside food and drink, glass containers, professional cameras with detachable
lenses, umbrellas, laser pointers, and weapons of any kind are refused at all
our venues. Sealed water bottles up to 500 millilitres are allowed at Kestrel
Amphitheatre only. Confiscated items are not stored or returned. Individual
organizers can add further restrictions for a specific event, and those appear
on the event page under Venue information a week before doors.

## Are medical bags and parent bags exempt

Yes. Bags carrying medication, medical equipment, or supplies for an infant are
exempt from the size limit at every venue. You do not need to register them in
advance or show documentation. Go to the accessible entrance rather than the
main gate, where staff will check the bag and let you through. Breast pumps,
insulin kits, and CPAP equipment are all covered by this exemption.

## Is there bag check or storage at the venue

Harbour Pavilion and Lumen Hall have paid bag check inside the main entrance,
at 10 dollars per item, open from doors until 30 minutes after the event ends.
Kestrel Amphitheatre and Northgate Arena have no bag storage of any kind. The
Foundry has free cloakroom space but only for coats, not bags. Bag check cannot
accept anything on the prohibited list, so an oversized bag with outside food
inside will still be refused.
